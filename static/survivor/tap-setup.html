Weather tap alert

Experimental. Off by default. After you enable this, four quick taps on a non-interactive weather area within 1.5 seconds save an emergency alert. There is no PIN or confirmation before sending.

Buttons, links, forms, search and PIN/setup dialogs do not count. Scrolling, dragging and long presses do not count. After an activation, leave 1.5 seconds without taps before another attempt.

It works only while the weather page is open, visible and focused. It does not work with your phone locked or the app closed. Accidental activations remain possible; this gesture has not been evaluated with users.

The alert saves without waiting for GPS. With the separate option below and active location-sharing consent, it may include a recent real GPS fix (up to two minutes old, accuracy within 1,000 metres) or add one shortly after receipt. A browser permission prompt may appear after saving starts. Denied, slow or offline GPS never stops the alert. Weather places are never shared as emergency GPS. No continuous tracking is used. The normal HELP button stays available.

“Saved on this phone” means it is waiting to send. “Received by Avenra” is not a promise of help. “Acknowledged by a responder” only confirms acknowledgement. Offline alerts retry through the existing queue when a connection returns and the app is open.
